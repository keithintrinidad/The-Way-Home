# The Way Home — An OCIA Journey

A companion for the Order of Christian Initiation of Adults (OCIA), for anyone journeying into the Catholic Church.

**Live:** https://keithintrinidad.github.io/The-Way-Home/

## What's inside

- **Journey**: the four periods of OCIA and the rites of each, tailored to candidates (already baptized) or catechumens (preparing for Baptism). Each rite has an explanation, your date, a checkbox when it's celebrated, and space for a reflection. A countdown shows the next step, and the usual Lent and Easter dates can be filled in automatically.
- **Sessions**: notes from each class, with Catechism paragraphs, Scripture, and questions to bring, gathered in one list until they're answered.
- **Saints**: explore and shortlist saints, note why each draws you, and choose a Confirmation saint.
- **Sponsor**: your sponsor's or godparent's contact details, notes from your conversations, and a prayer for them.

Also: light/dark mode, an About screen, an install panel, and offline-first startup for low-connectivity spaces.

## Privacy

Everything is stored on the device only (`localStorage`) and never sent anywhere. Export a backup, import one, or delete everything from *Settings and data* on the Journey screen. Backup files are not encrypted.

## A note on dates

The usual dates follow the Lent and Easter calendar, but every parish and diocese arranges its own, and not every parish celebrates every optional rite. Confirm with your OCIA team.

## Install

Open the live link on your phone and tap **Install**, or on iPhone choose *Share → Add to Home Screen*.
